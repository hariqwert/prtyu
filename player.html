Pure Direct vs. CORS Bypass Modes

Pure Direct: Plays 100% directly in your browser with zero server relay (works for CORS-friendly CDNs like Mux, Bitmovin, Cloudflare, etc.).

CORS Bypass: Built specifically for strict CDNs like Akamai (SonyLIV, Star, Jio, VLC feeds) that block web browser origins or demand specific user-agent headers.
